## Ranked issues and immediate fixes

**Critical:** No confirmed data leak, outcome drift, or arithmetic defect. The fixes below are necessary before stronger claims or another frozen run.

1. **Major — Mean-null validity, particularly H3/H4.** The centered sign flips require joint sign invariance at the boundary, typically justified by symmetric centered episode differences. Exact enumeration alone does not validate a composite mean null. For example, an H4 boundary distribution with zero difference 99% of the time and +200 errors 1% of the time has mean +2, yet all 48 zeros occur with probability 0.617. Those zeros yield a near-floor p and a zero bootstrap bound. Holm cannot repair invalid input p-values. The reported bounds are marginal, and pooled episode resampling varies the fixed panel quotas. With six U episodes and one nonzero H4 episode, tail coverage is unverified. **Fix now:** preserve decisions and bounds, label their assumptions and report observed episode distributions. Describe H3/H4 as conditional decisions under the frozen procedure. Drop unconditional safety wording. See CF1 `PLAN_CF1.md:49–73`, `cf1_analyze.py:21–56,86–96` and `CF1_RESULTS.md:19–36,62–69`.
2. **Major — Freeze portability and coverage.** 54/54 hashes match local bytes. 53/54 match committed blobs at both the freeze and result commits. The exception is `outputs/qshift_adaptive_robust_v1/ad3.py`: expected CRLF SHA-256 `98f5a62bc0b691c49a978278cea0bd19b15ae9d0c06ace614c224ff1b9520b4b`; committed LF SHA-256 `febfd05518fe610030a62b6074fe23af66410ef87cf5bae8c8bc00e697fcb368`. Converting the blob to CRLF exactly reproduced the expected hash. The guard hashes raw bytes, while `.gitattributes:3` requires LF. The freeze also omits lazy estimator sources (`ad1.py`, `brt.py`, `estimate2–5.py`) and runtime assets (`BRS_BURSTS.json`, `BRT_BURSTS.json`, `CLASSES_D5.json`, `NULL_THRESHOLD.json`). Checked omissions were unchanged across DB4, CF1 freeze and result Git objects, with equivalent local text. **Fix now:** record the mismatch in an addendum. For the next version, explicitly bind transitive inputs and environment, check actual freeze-commit membership, and test the hash guard on a fresh LF checkout. Preserve existing expected hashes. See CF1 `CF1_FREEZE.json:18–74`, `cf1_run.py:75–109,115–118`; `outputs/qshift_adaptive_robust_v1/studykit.py:65–94`, `ad2.py:97–101`, `ad1.py:50–66`; `outputs/qshift_d5_learned_support_v1/contract.py:45–51`, `c_contract.py:77–81`.
3. **Major — Narrow the scientific claims.** H1 compares against prespecified BP+OSD key A, selected by minimum equal-state mean training risk across five keys. It includes constant N_C, where the fixed C model ties W0F at 92 errors; A has 544. H2 compares whole policies that differ in next-state risk and hysteresis. H3's +2 is an average (maximum +6) on one A→E challenge. E is outside the bank yet represented in the learner's class catalog. Its 113 empty-P blocks used the learner 109 times and fallback four times. All 12 net excess errors vs AD3 occurred after the switch. **Fix now:** name the comparator and panel mixes, the labeled calibration, and the scope of the unknown-noise result. See CF1 `PLAN_CF1.md:54–59`, `CF1_RESULTS.md:42–70`; `outputs/qshift_decoder_bank_v1/db_train.py:88–93`, `db_config.py:27–50`; `outputs/qshift_decoder_bank_v2/db2_core.py:60–74`; `outputs/qshift_decoder_bank_v3/db3_config.py:24,46–53`.
4. **Major — Fallback benefit remains unproven.** DB4 had six branch visits, two key changes and one W0F-only error. CF1 had eleven visits, seven key changes and two W0F-only errors (U_e2, block 18). There were zero W0-only errors across both studies. W0 alone had 1,318 vs W0F's 1,320 errors in the H1 pool and tied at 663 in H2. **Fix now:** report the prespecified margin results and actual harms together. Describe the improvement on DB3 AA_e1 as retrospective development evidence. The static path handles estimator statuses, with no exception recovery in the decoder path. See DB4 `db4_core.py:15–24`, `DEV_RESULTS.md:45–57`; CF1 `CF1_RESULTS.md:66–69`.
5. **Minor — Harden validation and wording.** Analyzers check hashes and sums but lack exact packed-array width and key checks. CF1 catches all ordinary worker exceptions as retriable, despite the protocol's infrastructure-only retry rule. Neither issue changed these saved results. Add malformed-archive and code-error-stop tests, and a mocked invariance test that perturbs truth metadata, current-block calibration and evaluation labels while requiring unchanged adaptive decisions. Correct the descriptive DB3+DB4 episode count (39, versus the 41 DB2+DB3 replay episodes) and the “every regime” wording. See CF1 `cf1_analyze.py:60–82`, `cf1_run.py:134–151`, `PLAN_CF1.md:28–29,140–143`; DB4 `db4_run.py:303–325`, `tests/test_db4.py:105–115`, `PLAN_DB4.md:38,72–85`; `outputs/qshift_decoder_bank_v3/db3_config.py:29`.
