How does Q-SHIFT work?

Think of quantum error correction as finding mistakes in a message without reading the secret message itself.

01

Noise creates errors

Quantum information is sensitive. A burst can disturb multiple qubits, making the pattern of errors harder to interpret.

02

Detectors reveal clues

Special measurements produce a syndrome: signals that help identify errors without directly reading the stored logical information.

03

The decoder adapts

Q-SHIFT estimates the noise from detector data and adjusts matching weights to make better-informed correction decisions.
