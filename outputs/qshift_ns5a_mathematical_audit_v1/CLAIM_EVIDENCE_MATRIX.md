## Evidence classes

- **Proven (conditional):** algebraic identities and supermartingale arguments when assumptions in the row hold.
- **Numerically checked:** floating-point calculations, finite grids, finite differences, or saved deterministic probes.
- **Conditional:** a mathematically valid statement whose source-law/support assumptions have not been independently verified across all deployment cases.
- **Unverified:** finite-history estimator behavior, global identifiability, DEM transfer, false-switch count, real-time latency, and LER utility.

No stochastic result or decoder performance comparison was performed in this audit.
