## Proposed primary endpoint

Select **Endpoint B** before sampling because the intended claim concerns a practically worthwhile reduction:

- Estimand: equal-weight mean across p=.004,.008,.012,.016 of root-level paired `profile-adaptive LER − fixed nominal LER` differences; p=0 remains a safety stratum, not in this primary average.
- Null/alternative: \(H_0:\Delta\ge-0.0002\), \(H_1:\Delta<-0.0002\).
- Directional type-I error: one-sided alpha=.05, fixed before sampling.
- A practical-improvement claim requires the one-sided 95% upper confidence bound for \(\Delta\) to be strictly below −0.0002. A confidence bound that excludes zero but crosses −0.0002 supports evidence of some improvement, not the practical-threshold claim.
- Planning alternatives: explicitly show power at \(\Delta=-0.0003,-0.0004,-0.0005\), not only at the null boundary. The current 100-root allocation projects only ~46% power at −0.0003 and ~93% at −0.0004 under the provisional SD scenario.

If the owner instead chooses Endpoint A, replace the primary null with \(H_0:\Delta\ge0\); use the same one-sided alpha .05. Report −0.0002 only as a practical benchmark, with the estimate and interval relative to it. A and B must not be co-primary or selected after seeing outcomes.

## Proposed inferential procedure

1. Pair decoder predictions shot-for-shot. Reduce the paired errors within each independent root to one LER difference; do not treat shots as independent clusters.
2. Estimate \(\Delta=\frac14\sum_j\bar d_j\). Estimate variance \(\hat V=\frac1{16}\sum_j s_j^2/n_j\), allowing variance to differ by burst stratum.
3. Primary one-sided test and bound: Welch–Satterthwaite t inference over the four independent stratum means. Compute degrees of freedom as \(\nu=\hat V^2 / \sum_j[(s_j^2/(16n_j))^2/(n_j-1)]\). Report the t statistic, df, p-value, and matching one-sided 95% upper bound against −0.0002 (or zero if Endpoint A is approved).
4. Sensitivity: 100,000-replicate paired root-stratified bootstrap-t, resampling root differences with replacement independently within each stratum, preserving equal stratum weights. Compute \(T^*=(\Delta^* - \hat\Delta)/SE^*\); the one-sided upper bound is \(\hat\Delta-q_{.05}(T^*)SE(\hat\Delta)\). Use a new dedicated analysis seed recorded in the new freeze. Report percentile intervals descriptively. If primary Welch and bootstrap-t conclusions conflict, final decision is INCONCLUSIVE; do not choose the favorable method.
5. Keep the four nonzero stratum tests as one Holm-adjusted secondary family. Report estimates and intervals for all strata regardless of significance. The p=0 safety endpoint is a one-sided upper limit relative to +0.0002 and remains a required conjunct for overall success. No post-hoc additional condition claims.

For an overall development PASS under this proposal, require all of: the primary B one-sided Welch upper bound below −0.0002; Welch and bootstrap-t sensitivity conclusions not materially conflicting; each of the four nonzero-stratum point estimates below zero with none worse than +0.0002; and the p=0 one-sided upper limit no greater than +0.0002. The stratum direction/harm checks are a consistency gate, not proof of separate condition effects. Any condition-specific superiority statement additionally requires its Holm-adjusted test to pass. The changed primary boundary and inferential method are prospective replacements for the corresponding v2 primary rules; the other frozen conditions and comparisons remain as specified.

The sign-flip result may be retained as a labeled sensitivity analysis only, explicitly conditional on independent, sign-symmetric root differences under the null. It must not be called an exact randomization test or be the sole primary inference. The sampling design does not randomize decoder labels.

## Power and design implication

Power calculations must use root-level paired differences and show uncertainty in variance inputs. Existing per-stratum SD estimates are based on seven roots per source cell, so they are provisional. The saved inputs imply `SE = 0.0006429101 / sqrt(n)` for n roots per nonzero stratum, under stable independent-root variances. Endpoint B with n=100 has approximate powers .464, .929, .999 at true effects −.0003, −.0004, −.0005, respectively. To achieve 80% power for a 0.0001 gap beyond the margin requires ~256 roots per stratum (5.76M shots across five strata at the frozen 4,500 shots/root); this exceeds the existing 2.5M cap. The proposal therefore does **not** claim the frozen allocation is well-powered for modest effects.

Before any B-based sampling, the owner must either:

- authorize a prospectively revised sample/resource ceiling and root count based on an accepted target alternative and variance-risk sensitivity; or
- accept that the existing budget is exploratory for effects near −0.0003 and treat a null/inconclusive result as unable to rule out a smaller practical benefit.

No root count, seed inventory, or resource limit is changed by this document. A revised allocation would require a separate amendment, collision screen, runner/audit validation, and new pre-test freeze before generating any samples.
