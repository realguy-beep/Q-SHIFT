## Scientific/statistical clarifications for owner review

7. **Decide what “useful improvement” means.** The 45–50% relative reduction is aspirational only; the present gate tests directional evidence, not minimum practical benefit. Before any future confirmatory claim, specify an absolute or relative minimum worthwhile effect and the inferential target. Do not select the margin from V2 evaluation outcomes.
8. **Decide whether zero-burst harm needs a formal bound.** The three zero-burst cells are included in the overall equal-weight target, but there is no explicit non-harm/non-inferiority threshold for them. The “four of six negative” exploratory gate can allow a harmful cell. Either keep this explicitly as an exploratory all-cell average with no safety claim, or propose a separate prospective no-harm estimand and amendment.
9. **Keep power claims bounded.** V6 stored no labels or LER and so cannot estimate paired discordance or root variance. The 10-root/cell bootstrap has limited coverage assurance under skew, heterogeneity and zero inflation. If the aim remains feasibility, report uncertainty descriptively and do not claim the test is powered. If a confirmatory objective is desired, develop a separate design using independent development-root variability, prespecified type-I/coverage/power criteria, and likely more roots; never use this study's evaluation outcomes to plan a retrospective confirmatory claim.
10. **Keep the condition scope explicit.** The retained six cells are a finite, incomplete design. They estimate neither a Pauli-bias × burst interaction nor generalization to other burst geometries, strengths, code distances or hardware. Native gate/measurement rate asymmetry remains a separate study.
