# Q-SHIFT DB8 independent scientific, technical and provenance audit

**PASS WITH CONCERNS. NO ADVANCE is justified for both LA_R2 arms.** On eight U_NEAR episodes, the primary policy interpreted X09 as bank state B and overrode key A at onset. The override cost **375 extra logical errors**, with up to **55 in one block**. The secondary cap blocked one of those actions and still added 327 errors. The rule behaved as implemented. The failure exposes a limitation of the marginal goodness-of-fit score and of overriding the base decoder without modeling the risk from plausible out-of-bank states. The available evidence does not establish detector-level non-identifiability.

**Amendment A3 is acceptable for the amended development study, with a provenance correction.** It was committed before the final attempt and its filtered ranking reproduces. It changed the U_FAR challenge and reused earlier seeds. X03 data could be generated before its decoder failed, even though no U_FAR result artifact was retained. **The post-run analyser correction changes seed metadata validation only.** The independently calculated counts and decisions agree with the repaired analyser.

## U_NEAR mechanism

All eight recorded onsets had an alarm, single-block plausible set `{B}` and W0F key A. G is the temporal change alarm statistic; the B fit is the separate marginal deviance that is capped. The alarm threshold is 163.13. Both scores used detector rows only for the overlay. The inherited W0F base may use **past** labeled calibration. CB, LA_DB2 and R2 use the same current detector calibration budget and latency contract. W0F is the no-overlay reference with less information at current t. All arms score the same evaluation rows. Truth is used for generating data, ORACLE and diagnostic bookkeeping. The sampler-blocking truth-invariance test was inspected. It perturbs labels, truth metadata and future detectors, but its decoder-backed execution was stopped at the audit compute limit. See `db8_run.py:108–124,161–206`, `db8_core.py:8–30`, `tests/test_db8.py:231–296`.

The code evaluates `2n Σ KL(Bernoulli(kᵢ/n) || Bernoulli(pᵢ))` with n=2,000, fixed training templates and probability clipping. The implemented formula and observed cap logic are correct. This is a sum of marginal deviances with no covariance adjustment or decoder-risk weighting. For the approximate simulator DEM, parity-product marginals give λ=28.22956 for X09 vs the trained B template. Adding 120 detector terms gives the heuristic 148.22956. Independently summing the score expectation over each marginal binomial count gives **148.59728**. Within-shot correlation does not change that sum of expectations, but does change tails and the effective information. The model's detector correlation eigenvalues range from 0.356 to 2.729. No ill-conditioned inversion is used and no numerical defect was found. A chi-square tail approximation should not be treated as calibrated for these correlated features. Record-level fitted scores can be validated for selection consistency. Raw calibration detectors are absent, so their scores cannot be independently recalculated per episode.

**There is analytic discrimination headroom in the assumed model.** D117 has predicted firing rates 3.5063% for X09 and 2.5575% for true B. D117/D119 cofire rates are **1.3487% vs 0.4042%**. For two exactly known model hypotheses and IID shots, the equal-prior single-feature binomial overlap calculation gives **10.72%** error on the selected marginal and **0.87%** on the selected cofire. These are post-study, model-conditional diagnostics chosen using analytic moments, with no evaluation outcomes in feature selection. Their purpose is to refute exact observational equivalence under those modeled hypotheses. They do not validate a learned open-set classifier, robust parameters, hardware behavior or reduced logical errors. The current controller interprets a close fit to B as reason to override, without considering loss under nearby unknown supports. See `select_unknowns.py:35–61`, `db8_core.py:14–22`, `outputs/qshift_decoder_bank_v1/db_core.py:42–52` and the sampler-free model audit.

## Next investigation

First run a **sampler-free identifiability and risk study** using circuit/DEM moments. Fix a candidate feature family from geometry or known mechanism supports before inspecting any future outcomes. Compare marginals, selected pair cofires and joint parity moments under variation in background rate and burst support. Ask where the sets of plausible observables overlap **and whether the preferred decoder changes across the overlap**. Include designed hard cases close on both marginal and pair moments. Benchmark a prospective rule only after that analysis specifies information budgets and independent held-out supports.

| Direction | Information, cost, risk and evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Detector correlations | Same current detector rows. Sparse pair counts cost roughly O(shots × pairs); full pair enumeration is quadratic in detector count. Avoid choosing one favorable X09 pair and assuming transfer. Require analytic separation and calibrated uncertainty for an independently specified feature family. |
| Formal identifiability | Use parity signatures or moments and parameter envelopes of plausible noises. This is offline and sampler-free. Moment aliasing does not imply equality of all detector distributions. Document any ambiguity set and show risk differences under equally plausible models. |
| Sequential accumulation | Accumulate past/current detector evidence causally, with a declared delay and stopping rule. Cheap per block, but delays may forfeit B-onset gains and pooling can mix states. Require benefit under realistic dwell times at matched latency. |
| Robust, decision-focused selection and abstention | Retain a set of plausible noise models and override only if conservative loss against base supports it. The same detector stream can support this; offline loss models and uncertainty validation cost more. An overly narrow set misses unknowns and an overly broad one suppresses useful switching. Validate against good same-latency baselines with explicit harm and retention constraints on independent development supports. |

This investigation would leave hardware, within-block drift, larger distances and population safety open. No threshold or feature should be tuned to make these eight fits pass.
