# Deterministic validation record

## Execution boundary

Command: `python outputs/qshift_advanced_decoder_research_v2b/analyze_identifiability.py` from repository root, with Stim **1.16.0**. The program builds the existing circuit and DEM, enumerates categorical fault alternatives, and evaluates parity-character products. It uses no sampler, random-number generator, logical labels, decoder, training, seed inventory, or evaluation data. Runtime on the recorded execution was about 44 seconds.

## Independent algebraic cross-check

The primary implementation evaluates parity characters as a product over independent locations and applies the Boolean Fourier identity to detector moments. An independent reference propagates categorical parity-state distributions directly by XOR convolution. At four parameter points `(.008,.003)`, `(.008,.006)`, `(0,.003)`, and `(.020,.003)`, the maximum absolute difference between methods was between **1.98e-14 and 2.00e-14**; all comparisons passed absolute tolerance `1e-12`. This validates the deterministic expectation algebra, not the circuit model itself.

The two calculations share the same deterministic single-fault detector masks. Those masks were generated by setting one fault alternative at a time to probability one and converting that deterministic circuit; they were not sampled. This establishes the detector signatures under the Clifford Pauli-frame assumptions used by the builder. The original circuit’s categorical probabilities and the DEM approximation are then processed separately.

## Structural checks

- Circuit has 40 detector bits and one observable; extracted profile has 45 features.
- Enumerated 214 independent locations and 1,974 nonidentity categorical alternatives: 120 one-qubit, 1,800 two-qubit, 49 measurement and 5 burst alternatives.
- Five burst masks map exactly to `(D8,D10)`, `(D16,D18)`, `(D24,D26)`, `(D32,D34)`, `(D36,D38)`, matching pair features 40–44.
- With `approximate_disjoint_errors=False`, Stim rejects conversion of the original disjoint Pauli-channel alternatives, as expected. The repository setting `decompose_errors=True, approximate_disjoint_errors=True` yields 586 independent error instructions at burst p=0 and 591 at burst p=.008.
- At `(.008,.003)`, exact categorical vs approximate DEM feature means differ by maximum absolute **3.272e-5**, RMS **2.136e-5**. This is recorded as an approximation discrepancy, not as equivalence.

## Limitations

The algebraic cross-check is independent at the parity-state convolution layer but uses the same masks, so it does not independently validate the Stim circuit compiler or model specification. No stochastic validation was permitted or needed for the stated deterministic quantities. Full 40-bit syndrome distribution, higher-order feature moments beyond those needed for the covariance calculation, decoder behavior, logical error rate, finite-sample estimator accuracy and temporal adaptation are outside scope.
