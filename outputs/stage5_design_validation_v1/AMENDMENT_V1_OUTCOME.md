# Endpoint B candidate amendment v1 — outcome

Candidate amendment v1 is preserved as the prospective design that was evaluated. It is **not approved for sampling**.

The frozen design simulations found Welch type-I error of 7.57% in the right-skewed root-effect stress case, and bootstrap-t sensitivity simulations were too imprecise to certify an alternative. The 256-root allocation has about 80% power at true Δ=−0.0003 only under the point SD estimate; 1.5× between-root SD reduces that to about 51%. This does not meet a defensible readiness standard for a practical-benefit claim.

The separate pipeline benchmark and dynamic audit passed at 50 disposable roots/225k shots. They do not remedy the inferential issue. No Stage 5 data or sealed test data were used. Any revised statistical method/allocation must receive a new versioned proposal and pre-run freeze, with fresh design-simulation seeds; do not edit candidate v1 or the historical v2 protocol in place.
