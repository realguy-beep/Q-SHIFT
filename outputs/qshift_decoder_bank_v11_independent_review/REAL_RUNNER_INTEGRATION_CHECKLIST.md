# Real-runner integration gate

1. **Lifecycle before real draws.** Distinguish supervisor identity from lock contention or I/O failure. Close the recovery/startup gap and verify run-scoped worker quiescence, including idle and initializing children. Exercise startup and recovery with real Windows processes. Prohibit reader admission during ambiguous states. Make success imply full journal, commit, seed and policy validation.
2. **Canonical execution path.** Authorize an exact freeze commit through a study-specific launcher that fixes the job, decoder factory, preflight, sampler and analyzer entry points. Use a clean single-user local source snapshot, fresh controlled environment with declared import roots, and no stale bytecode. Revalidate actual module origins, runtime, configuration, training inputs and seed book before the first draw and when read. Do not expose general callback and pool injection to the execution entry point.
3. **Decoder and information contracts.** Pin circuit/DEM bytes or a reproducible identity, detector/observable order, backend modes and model information for each arm. Construct and exercise every frozen fixed key with deterministic fixtures. Predeclare failure handling for newly learned keys. Freeze allowed calibration, lookahead and oracle access, paired samples, disjoint partitions, and the no-label input boundary. Current-block detector-only lookahead implies one block of disclosed latency. Preserve the causal source windows and AD3 or fallback inputs needed by claimed endpoints.
4. **Sampling provenance.** Bind real calls to the exact stream, seed and expected row count. Journal intent durably before the call and validate returned shape, unique partition/row IDs and shared detector digest before confirming. Prevent more than one consumption of a seed and distinguish possible sampling from returned arrays and durable commits. Never retry a possibly sampled root automatically. Store raw or sufficient decision and pairing evidence appropriate to claims.
5. **Resources and interruption.** Run external whole-lifecycle supervision with fixed wall time, memory/disk headroom, reviewed end-to-end bounds, and OS process-tree cleanup. Treat v11's stall timeout as a separate guard. Preflight measured resources and rehearse root-stage and terminal failure injection without samples. Record exceptions, incomplete counts and sealed-data exclusions.
6. **Study-specific verification.** Preregister grids, baselines, estimator, claims, stopping rules and statistics. Freeze source, complete seed screen, training, analysis and evidence policy. Independently reproduce saved decisions and scoring. Obtain separate approval at that exact freeze before any development sampling. Confirmation and sealed work each require their own approval and evidence.
