# Q-SHIFT Stage 5 — Independent Confirmation Protocol Candidate

**State:** prospective candidate, prepared after Stage 4C and before any Stage 5 sample generation. This document is not an authorization to execute Stage 5. No Stage 5 shots have been generated.

## Selected decoder

Stage 4C selected the simpler Stage 4B detector-profile estimator plus PyMatching; the CNN extension is dropped. Freeze the Stage 4C refitted Stage 4B profile means/scales and grid (`FROZEN_PROFILE_ESTIMATOR.json`) as the single deployable adaptive candidate. The profile uses p knots {0,0.01,0.02}, detector-only marginal/cofire features, linear profile interpolation, and 0.00001 probability grid. At inference it receives a separate 2,000-shot calibration detector batch and rebuilds the decoder through the Stage 4C/Stage 4B Stim DEM and PyMatching method. It never receives generating p, event flags, evaluation detectors, or observables. Keep nominal p=0 PyMatching as the strongest fixed baseline. A true-p PyMatching arm is privileged and diagnostic only.

The selection is development-only: in Stage 4C the profile method produced 478/87,500 errors versus fixed nominal's 545/87,500; the CNN produced 482 and did not improve on profile. Stage 4C did not confirm generalization or hardware behavior.

## Design and allocations

Use the same synthetic mechanism only: rotated Z-memory, d=3, r=5, 40 detectors/one observable; balanced categorical gate p=0.003, measurement p=0.003, bias ratio 10; independent per-round `CORRELATED_ERROR(p) X8 X10`; p={0,0.004,0.008,0.012,0.016}. Stim DEM conversion remains `decompose_errors=True, approximate_disjoint_errors=True`; PyMatching correlation handling is enabled. No noise family, code distance, or decoder setting may be selected after Stage 5 labels are viewed.

Per condition use 100 independent roots, each with 2,000 calibration shots and 2,500 paired evaluation shots. The five conditions give 500 roots, 1,000 independent sampling seeds, 1,000,000 calibration shots, 1,250,000 evaluation shots, and **2,250,000 unique Stage 5 shots**. The profile templates are frozen Stage 4C development model parameters; Stage 5 does not refit the estimator. The three arms decode the same evaluation detector rows and are scored against the same observable rows. Training/validation partition is not needed for this frozen statistical estimator.

Proposed prospective seed range: 4,240,000,001–4,240,001,002 (1,000 sampling seeds plus two analysis RNGs), checked against 3,811 distinct values found in explicitly enumerated historical development seed metadata, including Phase 4A/4B configs, checkpoints and per-seed summaries, plus all 554 Stage 4C seeds. `seed_inventory.json` and `seed_audit.json` preserve the exact mapping and hashes of each examined metadata file. The 1,000 shot seeds are split into 500 calibration streams followed by 500 paired-evaluation streams; the two final seeds are assigned to bootstrap and sign-flip statistics. Unrecorded historical RNG use cannot be ruled out. No current sealed-test file is searched or opened.

## Power and resources

Using the larger observed Stage 4B/Stage 4C root-level SD in each of the four nonzero conditions (0.0008142, 0.0009641, 0.0010690, 0.0019693), the estimated standard deviation of the equal-stratum mean at 100 roots/condition is approximately 0.0000643. This calculation uses the conservative per-root SD without reducing it for more shots. A normal approximation gives roughly 87.5% power to reject zero for a true effect of −0.0002, but the gate also requires the point estimate to be at most −0.0002; at the exact boundary that point-estimate requirement has only about 50% probability. At a prespecified planning alternative of −0.0005, that point-estimate threshold is expected to be exceeded with high probability if the SD estimate is representative. These are rough planning calculations based on only seven roots per development condition, not a guarantee. If an independently reviewed power simulation raises a concern, revise before generating samples.

Hard execution limits: 2,500,000 unique shots; 1,800 seconds wall time; process RSS below 2 GiB; CPU only. The allocation uses 2.25 million shots, leaving a 250,000-shot ceiling margin. Based on Stage 4B's 175,500-shot/59-second and Stage 4C's 277,500-shot/10.3-second runs, the estimate is deliberately conservative and should be benchmarked with deterministic no-shot checks only before any authorized sampling. Store root evidence atomically and retain all completed roots on interruption.

## Endpoints and frozen decision rules

Primary estimand: equal-weight mean over the four nonzero-p condition means of the root-paired LER difference (frozen profile-adaptive PyMatching minus fixed nominal PyMatching). For each Monte Carlo assignment, independently draw a ±1 sign for every root difference within each condition, recompute the four condition means, then take their equal-weight mean. The two-sided statistic is the absolute value; count ties as extreme and use the plus-one p-value correction. Generate 100,000 assignments with NumPy `Generator(PCG64(4240001002))`; consume the same stream in this order: primary statistic, then the four per-condition tests in ascending p order. This is a paired root sign-symmetry test, not a treatment randomization test; interpretation assumes root-level effects are sign-symmetric under the null.

For the 20,000-resample percentile bootstrap, resample 100 roots with replacement independently within each condition, recompute the equal-weight primary statistic, and use the 2.5th/97.5th percentiles. For the zero-burst harm check, resample roots only from p=0 and use its 95th percentile as the one-sided upper limit. Use `Generator(PCG64(4240001001))`, consuming draws first for the primary bootstrap and then for p=0. Holm-adjust the four prespecified condition-specific sign-flip p-values. Ties in per-root decoder error counts do not favor either policy; they remain zero differences in the estimand. The primary and secondary inferential unit is the independent root, not the shot. Report raw counts, paired candidate-only/fixed-only failures, per-root effects, and per-condition effects.

Development gate carried forward before sampling: (1) primary mean difference ≤−0.0002 absolute LER; (2) primary 95% root-bootstrap upper bound <0; (3) primary sign-flip p<0.05; (4) all four nonzero condition means favor the adaptive arm and none has harm >+0.0002; (5) at p=0 the one-sided 95% root-bootstrap upper bound is ≤+0.0002. The four nonzero condition tests are secondary and Holm-adjusted. Failure is a negative confirmation; no expansion or replacement conditions may be added post hoc.
