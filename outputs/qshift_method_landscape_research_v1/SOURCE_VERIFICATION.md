## Checkpoint 2 source verification addendum

- **Primary sources opened:** DGR arXiv full HTML (`arXiv:2311.16214v3`), drifting-noise estimation arXiv full HTML (`arXiv:2511.09491`) and publisher page (PRX Quantum 7, 033024, 2026-08-06), Remm et al. arXiv HTML (`arXiv:2502.17722`), QAdapt arXiv full HTML v2 (`arXiv:2607.28422v2`, 2026-11-10), Page DOI record, ADWIN original author PDF, Adams–MacKay author PDF, official BeliefMatching GitHub landing page.
- **Specific verified corrections:** The drifting-estimation work is peer-reviewed/published as of the publisher page, not just a preprint. Its displayed estimator equations assume independent graph-event mechanisms and model only decoding graphs. The relative-window method estimates instantaneous edge rates using two overlapping windows of sizes W and W+1. The Q-SHIFT injected burst is itself independent across round boundaries, so the method may be relevant after a justified graphlike mapping; the paper does not establish mapping, nuisance separation or accuracy for Q-SHIFT's full categorical-gate circuit.
- **QAdapt semantics:** Full source explicitly trains on simulated correction targets, processes named sequential noise tasks with online EWC, then evaluates frozen terminal weights on unseen compound conditions and hardware records. This differs from unsupervised detector-only causal graph switching. QAdapt's own paper reports a 650,332-parameter architecture and requires task update data.
- **DGR semantics:** Full-text method says prior MWPM matchings are counted as pseudo observations; updated edge weights are used in subsequent MWPM, with correlation reweighting for difficult cases. The authors state sample demand depends on physical rate and show convergence plots around one million trials for a particular p=0.001 case. This is a warning about history length and feedback, not a Q-SHIFT resource estimate.
- **Not verified yet:** All theorem details, complete experimental settings, source-code revision/license for DGR and drifting-noise estimator, full BeliefMatching upstream compatibility against current pinned package, exact drift-estimator robust variance in the small Q-SHIFT regime.

**Citation-chain and theorem update:** Verified Wagner et al. publisher metadata and full arXiv theorem. The exact generic condition is local independent channel supports with every pairwise union support detectable (plus nonzero/trivial-probability conditions); measurement bits enter via a data-syndrome code. The support-size corollary is `floor((d_p-1)/2)` combined qubits/measurement bits. This does not prove or disprove the constrained Q-SHIFT two-parameter burst/measurement model; instantiate the theorem for the actual circuit before interpreting it. The local feasibility note has an incorrect DOI date suffix (`09-22`); correct DOI is `09-19`. Historical note is not edited.
