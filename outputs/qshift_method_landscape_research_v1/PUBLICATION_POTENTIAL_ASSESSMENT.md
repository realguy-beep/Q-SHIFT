# Publication Potential Assessment

## Current position

The saved work supports a rigorous deterministic methods/model-identifiability paper concept, but not a claim that Q-SHIFT's adaptive decoder improves logical performance. A defensible eventual contribution could combine (i) exact categorical-circuit detector-feature derivations, (ii) a transparent mapping and limitations for syndrome edge-moment estimation, and/or (iii) a state-valid sequential alarm with nuisance-robust guarantees, followed by a separately authorized paired development evaluation.

## Prior-art overlap

Direct overlap exists with syndrome-based drift estimation (Bhardwaj et al.; Spitz et al.; Huo and Li), syndrome-correlation calibration (Remm et al.), DGR reweighting, Bayesian syndrome inference, QAdapt, supervised calibration-conditioned decoders, decoder-model compatibility/postselection, detector-statistics learning, e-processes, and hardware adaptation. A paper must state a precise technical distinction and benchmark against the closest equal-information method. “Adaptive decoding” alone is not novel.

## Evidence required before a submission claim

1. Independent derivation/review of exact categorical feature laws and continuous nuisance envelopes.
2. Correct and independently audit the checkpoint-133 fixed-point threshold predicate and any future fixed-point lambda bank.
3. Fully specify a bidirectional/abstaining state machine with valid repeated false-switch control.
4. Freeze practical margin, horizon, calibration/tuning allocation, root-level paired analysis and stopping rules.
5. Obtain development-only results on fresh streams with strong static, periodic, trailing-window and EWMA baselines, including unconditional LER and paired risk.
6. Account for graph-bank construction, online update, decoder inference, memory and calibration overhead.
7. Independently replicate and clearly separate categorical-circuit and DEM-generated studies.

## Realistic assessment

A deterministic theory/software paper may be possible if independently reviewed and technically narrowed; publication potential is **plausible but unproven**. A performance paper is premature. The historical adaptive result is explicitly nonconfirmatory and below its historical practical benchmark; it cannot support a positive claim. No venue, acceptance, or novelty outcome is promised.
