# Distance-5 intermediate schedule: inconclusive

For aligned d5 Z memory, seven active rounds plus two p=.003 biased data-idle cycles produced 140 failures in 1,000,000 shots; nine active rounds produced 166. The rate ratio was .843 (95% log-normal interval .674–1.056). The point estimate favors the intermediate schedule, but the interval includes no difference and fails the frozen advancement gate requiring RR<=.90 with upper confidence bound below 1.

The candidate had 168 detectors, 560 two-qubit pair sites, and 218 one-qubit Pauli targets including 50 idle targets; the nine-round arm had 216 detectors, 720 pair sites, and 216 one-qubit targets. Both had the same 64-qubit geometry, terminal readout, and nine-cycle duration. Preflight checks passed; projected main time was 12.5 seconds, measured total 15.2 seconds.

This is not evidence of a d5 schedule gain. The frozen gate failed, so no additional round-count point or follow-up sample expansion was run. Combined with the d5 five-plus-idle result, the d3 schedule lead does not currently scale into a robust d5 recommendation.
