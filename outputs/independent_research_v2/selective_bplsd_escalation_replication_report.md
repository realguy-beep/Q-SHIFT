# Selective BP+LSD escalation: independent efficacy replication

The fresh stream replicated the accuracy signal. Across six d3 conditions, equal-weight macro LER fell from .005213 to .004533, a 13.0% reduction. The correlation-versus-ordinary matching flag selected 1,530/600,000 samples (0.255%); all BP+LSD escalated syndromes were valid. Five conditions improved; X-memory/X-bias was effectively tied (131 vs 132 errors). No condition was harmed by more than 5%.

The full policy remained slow: mean total decode time was 8.24x fixed correlation-enabled matching, including both matching passes and BP+LSD calls. The advancement gate therefore fails again. This independently verifies a useful ambiguity signal but rejects this implementation as a practical decoder policy. A future candidate would need a substantially faster local escalator and its own frozen protocol; no tuning of BP+LSD was performed.
