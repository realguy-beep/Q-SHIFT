# Limitations and unresolved evidence

1. **Simulation only.** Stim and PyMatching produced all reported data. No physical quantum processor or hardware integration was tested.
2. **Narrow circuit and noise family.** The study uses rotated Z-memory, distance 3, five rounds, and the frozen synthetic spatial-burst model. It does not establish performance for other distances, memory bases, devices, or noise processes.
3. **Comparator scope.** The deployable comparison is detector-profile-adaptive PyMatching versus the frozen nominal fixed PyMatching model. The privileged true-probability reference receives information unavailable to a deployable decoder. This study does not compare against every possible static decoder calibrated to burst noise, and no claim is made that the privileged reference is mathematically optimal.
4. **Statistical scope.** The secondary Holm procedure is calibrated over six prespecified root-effect location-scale families. Its guarantees are conditional on that finite family and independent-root assumptions, not distribution-free.
5. **Raw arrays are local.** The 6,050 compressed detector/observable/prediction archives are excluded from GitHub. Per-root audit records, archive hashes, aggregate results, execution provenance, and finalization manifests are included. Public package verification can reproduce aggregate counts and frozen statistics, but not re-decode raw arrays.
6. **Auditor status.** The saved-array auditor is an internal independent implementation; no external third-party audit has been performed.
7. **Historical safety-output deviation.** The expected SHA-256 of the original v3.4.2 `SAFETY_TEST_RESULTS_V4.json` is `d191d48d999bf169cbcaa5a9e42fb604e8241f387bee84e19c598402dc319a4c`; the current named copy is `3d0aa61720a2f7258da661d48163d02bdaba3cc3e22457c97799c69c30b3f64c`. The same-named v3.4 and v3.4.1 copies hash to `672f19b73a95f6f3ebb76d90fcd1cc0aa6ed49484cd727ff8ac8b3691a3ce930` and `e1b153761b81098a47fd596b9011e1f26987d614fe3079e5040da38b4bfa9bde`. The historical bytes were not reconstructed or overwritten. This affects byte-level verification of an earlier safety-test report, not the Stage 5 scientific arrays or the completed Stage 5 independent audit.
8. **Recovery provenance redaction.** The original recovery provenance contains absolute Windows user-directory paths and is therefore not copied verbatim. `RECOVERY_PROVENANCE_REDACTED.json` preserves the relative source locations, inventory hash, file counts, recovery flag, and SHA-256 of the original provenance file.

No omitted, unavailable, or unverified evidence is described as recovered.
