# Proposed preregistration for response-gap validation

**Status.** Design proposal. No sampling, production seed generation, freeze or execution took place in this sprint. It requires a separate build and authorization, followed by a hash-pinned freeze before any held-out outcomes. The design concerns public development noise. “Held-out” here means fresh development episodes and layouts withheld from policy fitting, with no access to frozen independent-test material.

## Estimands and claims

Primary stratum R targets the repeated weak burst and compares one frozen directional selector with both AD3 and the strongest deployable fixed decoder. Estimate unconditional paired whole-episode LER difference over equally weighted R episodes. Co-primary delay endpoint is the episode mean fraction of **recurrent ON opportunities with the correct effective action by the next eligible block and before the next true change**. An action counts if the selected decoded model key agrees with a predeclared acceptable support-and-flip family, including the permitted background tolerance. Report the analogous first ON and OFF rates, state uncertainty, and actual model-change times. Analyze changed rows from the onset, including the first scored block that precedes any possible reactive change. No postselection by alarm, accepted model or decoder agreement.

The primary practical margin is a proposed absolute reduction of .001 versus each baseline, one logical error per 1,000 evaluated rows. Unmargined and .0005/.002 sensitivities are descriptive. Require a static noninferiority upper limit of +.0005 versus robust fixed on the static mixture. These operational margins require owner acceptance before freeze. For timely recurrent-ON success, require point gain ≥.20 versus AD3 and paired evidence of a positive gain. Future broad claims require further tests of novel layouts, code sizes and noise, and separate confirmation.

## Episode generation and same information

At unchanged whole-block cadence, use d=5/five rounds, q=.01 when ON unless specified, 24 blocks with 0–5 warm-up and 6–23 scored. Each block allocates 2,000 detector-only, 250 separately labelled calibration and 1,000 evaluation rows. Draw fresh root identities independently within each schedule stratum, then derive distinct child seeds by block/stream, checking collisions against authorized inventories. Sampling and scoring are performed once, shared row-for-row across all arms in an episode. Independent evaluation observables remain scorer-only. No root, waveform or seeded replay from historical AD2/AD3 enters the evaluation. Controllers receive only completed calibration history and eligible decoder row batches. The clock can enforce causal order and dwell counting. It cannot expose true schedules, onset indices, support, generating parameters or scoring labels.

| Panel | Conditions known to generator and scorer only | Allocation |
|---|---|---:|
| Null | Full static episodes drawn independently from a declared uniform mixture of OFF and public ON states A–D at p=.003. Separate dev-only stress covers shifted stationary p. | 30 roots |
| R primary | A-like weak ON/OFF. First ON index uniformly 6 or 7. Three successive dwell lengths independently uniform on {4,5}, which creates first ON, first OFF, recurrent ON and final OFF. Each policy starts without episode history before warm-up. | 12–24 roots |
| K | Stable starts with a single characterised A→B or B→C relocation, onset uniformly 8–12. | U | Relocation to a public support excluded from every fitted policy bank/template and from policy selection. Geometry-only class enumeration by the unchanged baseline learner is allowed. Freeze three support/episode pairs at each of two nuisance settings, with onset uniformly 8–12. | 6 roots |

K/U/T are stress and descriptive panels at this scale. Keep nuisance and dwell manifests inaccessible to policies. In U the held-out supports are named in the experiment's scorer manifest, not in any fitted bank. In R the public candidate universe can include A. Give both AD3 and the candidate identical approved advance model information and budget in the matched-information comparison. Also retain the original AD3 state logic as a benchmark. An offline bank/library prefill changes the baseline's initialization, so log a distinct arm and keep original AD3 unchanged. A gain against original cold start alone does not isolate the detection algorithm. On the separate cold-start comparison start every selector with a common broad background and empty support cache, and permit only post-observation model construction.

## Frozen arms and selection

- **Primary candidate.** The single bank selector and fallback from [Recommended approach](RECOMMENDED_APPROACH.md). Predeclare feature masks, fit/calibration order, key cap, distinct OFF/ON thresholds, tie order, ambiguity, dwell, expiry, risk margins and nominal/default behavior. Calibrate false-switch risk on complete development episodes, running all restarts. A parameter grid proposal is training-only ridge fractions {.05,.20,.50} of average feature variance after scaling, dwell {0,1,2} eligible looks, and max-bank training objective “fewest misses before next change subject to stationary episode false-switch rate ≤.10, followed by lowest paired whole-episode LER and then smaller model.” Freeze selected thresholds/margins, model hashes and fitting roots. For thresholds search observed development score quantiles {.90,.95,.975,.99,1} from the dedicated tuning split and include no switch. If the grid is too sparse, revise under a new development proposal before viewing held-out results.
- **Original AD3 and AD.** Use the pinned rules with the new common horizon and observation budget. AD3 uses recorded τ and M, empty episode library and AD fallback, AD uses six-block E1T and nominal fallback. Use a source-faithful reimplementation test against fixed chronology fixtures and reconcile LF/CRLF hashes before authorization. Also compare an AD3-style recall arm with the same prebuilt bank and outputs available as the candidate, frozen before the trial.
- **Strong static.** From the shared bank, select one fixed BP+OSD key using the lowest pooled development paired unconditional LER across the prespecified equally weighted null, R, K and T design panels. Fixed windows and a finite, pinned background/support grid include nominal, OFF, one model per characterised support, and approved pooled models if construction is valid. Tie break smaller cost then stable key order. Choose the argmin only with development labels. This reflects a deployment prior and supervised development resource, never current hidden noise. Include a frozen correlated PyMatching static selected by the same criterion within its engine as a secondary cross-engine comparison. Report actual model identities and if two arms produce identical predictions.
- **Lightweight baselines.** Freeze cadence-matched trailing-window and EWMA score selectors (window {2,3,6} blocks, EWMA gain {.2,.4,.6}, same bank, eligibility and risk machinery), plus periodic six-block and ONCE AD fits. Include hold-last-accepted fallback ablation. Equivalent arms share compute when keys and inputs are identical. Log standalone cost estimates separately from deduplicated harness cost.
- **Privileged reference.** True current support and background BP+OSD, clearly separated from deployable evidence. The gap against it measures model-information headroom in that engine.

Training, policy tuning and frozen evaluation episodes are disjoint. Training estimates feature statistics and paired action-loss tables. Use the tuning split for selecting one candidate and comparator configurations by the fixed rules above. A cheap diagnostic should compare raw class/flip correctness before guard, accepted correctness, model-key differences and paired errors. If all candidate actions give nearly identical predictions, do not escalate on alarm speed alone. Keep the training/tuning window reuse entirely within roots. Do not optimize on stress-panel outcomes after their release.

## Event rules and risk

- Determine matching offline from a frozen schedule scorer. Alarm index is the last observed block that contributed to the first eligible alarm. Effective switch index is the first scored block using the changed key. Match an event at most once, to the first eligible alarm or correct action in its own transition interval, separately for each type. A correct key already active at onset is “prepositioned” and scored separately. Record actionable latency zero only for genuinely prepositioned actions and report whether the action was valid just before the transition. For the primary timely fraction, require the acceptable key by c+1 (whether prepositioned or switched) and report the result with prepositioned events excluded as sensitivity. A flag alone never proves OFF unless the chosen decoder is an allowed OFF action. Freeze acceptable key sets and OFF criteria based on generating model and representations, before scoring. Keep unrelated key changes and correct proxy actions distinct.
- Save full empirical cumulative fractions by delay and missed by event end. Report counts of pulse hits before expiry, first ON, recurrent ON, OFF, background-only, correct-state stability and wrong-key activations. Keep misses in denominators. A median exceeding support is reported as unattained rather than imputed. Report paired transition-block and whole-episode LER, settled LER, prediction disagreement and direction of disagreement, false alarms per eligible look, false action changes per episode, and burst ON/OFF results. A change on a static episode is false by the primary control metric. Define false action there as a transition between distinct action keys after the initial scored action, excluding duplicate selection of the current key; include fallback entry and exit. A presence alarm on a background-only step is a diagnostic, and a burst-support action change is an error unless semantically matched. Log switches, repeated alarms, risk abstentions, dwell suppression and label availability.
- Safety gate uses **any false action change per 24-block stationary episode** of the declared mixture with exact one-sided 95% binomial upper limit ≤.10. Thirty independent mixture roots with zero false switches have upper limit \(1-.05^{1/30}=.0950\). A failure defeats this safety claim. Per-state rare safety is unresolved at that allocation. False alarms and alert-only codes have separate counts. For changes monitor wrong switches and unmatched alarms, separating conditional-on-event rates and episode incidence. ## Inference, sizing and resource gates

For paired LER each episode gives \((e_{\text{candidate}}-e_{\text{baseline}})/(18\times1{,}000)\). Estimate the mean and one-sided 97.5% Student t bounds on the independent episode differences for R and the null as separate populations. For non-primary pooled summaries use equal stratum weights with independent variance terms or report all strata separately. Report a whole-episode stratified percentile bootstrap as a sensitivity, plus leave-one-root-out point estimates and the count of discordant roots. Inference is conditional on frozen calibration. Student t coverage relies on approximately normal episode means and is uncertain with few, lumpy episodes. If LER differences have zero variance, fewer than four episodes have nonzero paired differences, or one root determines whether a bound clears its gate, classify that LER endpoint as inconclusive. Never use shot-level or transition-level bootstrap. [E12](REFERENCES.md#primary-literature)

R has one recurrent ON event per root, so use a one-sided exact paired binomial test for timely success versus AD3. Conditional on the number of discordant roots, test candidate-only wins against Binomial(discordant roots, .5). Report a conservative 95% lower confidence bound for the timely rate gain from the one-sided 97.5% lower exact bound on candidate-only win proportion minus the one-sided 97.5% upper exact bound on AD3-only win proportion, both with all R roots as denominator. Use the paired test for the gate and the bound to convey precision. No discordant roots gives no evidence of improvement. [E12](REFERENCES.md#primary-literature)

Promote only if upper paired LER bounds against **both** primary comparators are below −.001 on R, timely fraction gain versus AD3 has one-sided paired p≤.025 and point improvement ≥.20, and the null passes both safety and LER upper limit +.0005. These all-required tests form one conjunction for this promotion. Separate superiority claims among descriptive arms/panels need fresh validation or multiplicity control. Treat insufficient precision as inconclusive. Secondary panels screen for credible failure modes and cannot establish a general improvement. Prespecify null and S2-like contrasts before seed generation. For an approximate sizing check with desired clearance \(\delta_{\rm gap}\) between planning effect and decision margin, \(n\approx(1.96+.842)^2\sigma_{\rm episode}^2/\delta_{\rm gap}^2\). At hypothetical SD .002 and clearance .002 this gives about 8 roots, and at clearance .001 about 32. These are normal approximations, not formal power for the conjunction. For the timely endpoint, six candidate-only improvements and zero AD3-only improvements give one-sided p=.015625. Use only development variability, finite-cluster sensitivity and expected discordance to choose n in range and declare the detectable gap. If required n exceeds the cap for a necessary endpoint, stop after the development screen or label any approved continuation as feasibility only.

## Failure, exposure and optional prefix stage

Stop on row-pairing, chronology, hash, decoder-identity, seed or data-split mismatch, run/time/RAM cap breach, or invalid predictions. Preserve complete and partial records with episode status and timestamps. Permit resume only with the same authorized inputs and write-once verified root outputs. Score every completed evaluation row in the matched primary denominator. If a primary root is incomplete, report it and classify the frozen primary comparison as incomplete. Never drop selected failures or peek at results to extend roots. Freeze blinded monitoring, stop checks, endpoint code, thresholds and exact retry conditions before execution.

Only after block-level evaluation, a separate optional branch can split the same budget per block into four ordered chunks of 500 detector rows, 62/63 calibration labels (two chunks each), then 250 independent scored rows. An action can use only data delivered up to the preceding observation cutoff for its scored batch. Define pulse onsets with a separate generator clock and vary offsets. Share the same chunk streams between fine-cadence and coarse-cadence versions. Include untimely decisions, simultaneous label delays, extra repeated looks and the resource overhead. Freeze a separate attribution window and re-calibrate false-switch risk. A prefix comparison tests whether shorter observation batches improve net LER, while controlling for new data access and altered chronology.
